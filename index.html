About Me

I am a Researcher, Educator & Innovator in Artificial Intelligence, Image Processing, and Cybersecurity. Holding First Class with Distinction degrees at both Bachelor's and Master's levels, my career spans technical instruction, IT infrastructure management, and specialized threat training.
